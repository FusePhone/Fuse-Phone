Frequently Asked Questions

How much does interior painting cost in Nassau County?

Interior painting costs vary based on room size, prep work needed, and paint quality. We offer free on-site estimates with transparent, no-surprise pricing. Most rooms range from $800-$1,200 depending on size and complexity.

How long does it take to paint a room?

A standard room typically takes 1-2 days including prep work, priming, and two coats of paint. Larger projects or rooms requiring extensive prep may take longer. We'll provide a clear timeline during your estimate.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes! We provide free on-site estimates for all projects in Nassau County, Brooklyn, and Long Island. We'll assess your space, discuss your vision, and provide a detailed, written quote with no obligation.

What paint brands do you use?

We use premium paints from Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams — the industry's most trusted brands. We recommend low-VOC options that are safe for your family and deliver beautiful, long-lasting color.

Do you serve Brooklyn?

Yes! Gama Interior Painting now has an office in Brooklyn at 41 Schermerhorn St #1018, Brooklyn, NY 11201. We serve all Brooklyn neighborhoods including Brooklyn Heights, Park Slope, Williamsburg, DUMBO, and more. Learn more about our Brooklyn painting services.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. Gama Interior Painting is fully licensed and insured. We carry comprehensive liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage for your complete peace of mind.
