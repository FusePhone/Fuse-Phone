Common Questions from Park Slope Homeowners

How much does it cost to paint a Park Slope brownstone? +
Most rooms run $800–$1,200 depending on size and prep work needed. Every project is different — ceiling height, plaster condition, and scope all factor in. We provide a detailed written estimate broken down by room so you know exactly what you're paying for. No surprises, no hidden fees. All prices include premium Benjamin Moore paint, thorough prep, and our 2-year warranty.
Do you have experience with prewar brownstones? +
Absolutely. Park Slope brownstones are a major part of our business. We understand plaster walls, high ceilings, intricate moldings, and the lead paint considerations that come with pre-1978 homes. We're EPA RRP certified for lead-safe work practices.
Is the paint safe for kids and babies? +
Yes. We use low-VOC and zero-VOC Benjamin Moore paints by default. Rooms are safe to sleep in the same night in most cases. We also use EPA lead-safe practices in older homes to prevent any lead dust exposure.
Can you provide a COI for my co-op or condo board? +
Yes, we provide Certificates of Insurance the same day, customized to your building's requirements. We also coordinate with management on elevator bookings, building access, and noise hour restrictions.
How long does a typical apartment or brownstone take? +
A single room takes 1–2 days. A full 2-bedroom apartment is typically 3–4 days. A full brownstone floor can be 4–6 days depending on the scope. We'll give you a detailed timeline with your estimate.
What's included in the free estimate? +
We visit your home, take measurements, discuss colors and scope, and deliver a detailed written proposal typically within 24 hours. It breaks down every room, surface, and coat so you know exactly what you're getting. No surprises.
What areas of Park Slope do you cover? +
All of Park Slope — from Flatbush Avenue to Prospect Park West, Grand Army Plaza down to Greenwood. We also serve South Slope, Windsor Terrace, Prospect Heights, Gowanus, Carroll Gardens, and all surrounding Brooklyn neighborhoods. Our Brooklyn office is at 41 Schermerhorn St #1018.
