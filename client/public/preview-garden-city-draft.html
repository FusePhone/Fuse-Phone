Why Garden City Homeowners Choose Us

Garden City is one of Long Island’s most sought-after villages, known for its tree-lined streets, historic architecture, and homeowners who take pride in maintaining their properties. Many homes here were built in the early-to-mid 1900s, featuring plaster walls, ornate crown molding, and detailed trim work that require a painter who understands how to work with — not against — these classic elements.

Whether your Colonial needs a full interior refresh or your Tudor has detailed woodwork that demands precision, we bring the Hamptons-trained craftsmanship that Garden City homes deserve. Our crew handles plaster repair, ceiling crack filling, and meticulous trim work that other painters avoid.

Garden City Painting FAQs

How much does interior painting cost in Garden City?

Garden City homes often have higher ceilings, plaster walls, and detailed trim — all of which affect pricing. A standard room typically runs $800–$1,200 depending on prep work needed. Larger projects with skim coating or crown molding painting are quoted separately. We provide free, detailed on-site estimates so you know exactly what to expect.

Do you repair plaster walls in Garden City homes before painting?

Yes, plaster repair is one of our core specialties. Many Garden City homes have aging plaster with cracks, chips, or uneven surfaces. We skim coat, sand, and prime all plaster before applying paint to ensure a smooth, lasting finish.

How close are you to Garden City?

Our headquarters is at 206 Mayfair Ave, West Hempstead, NY 11552 — roughly a 10-minute drive from Garden City. We’ve completed numerous projects in the village and surrounding streets.

How long does a full home interior painting take in Garden City?

A standard 3-bedroom Garden City colonial typically takes 3–5 days depending on surface conditions and prep work. Homes with plaster repair, high ceilings, or detailed trim may take a full week. We’ll give you a clear timeline at your estimate.

Can you paint crown molding and detailed trim?

Absolutely. Garden City’s colonials and Tudors often have ornate crown molding, chair rails, and window casings. Our painters have the precision and patience for this type of detailed work — sharp lines, smooth finishes, no shortcuts.

Do you handle older Garden City homes with lead paint?

Yes, we’re EPA Lead-Safe Certified. Many Garden City homes were built before 1978 and may have lead-based paint layers. We follow all EPA-mandated safety protocols to protect your family during any prep or painting work.

What areas near Garden City do you also serve?

We serve all of Nassau County including New Hyde Park, Mineola, Stewart Manor, Floral Park, West Hempstead, Franklin Square, and beyond. We also work in Brooklyn and surrounding Long Island communities.
