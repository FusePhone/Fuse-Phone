Professional Wallpaper Removal

Done Right, Without Damaging Your Walls

New Hyde Park Painting FAQs

How much does interior painting cost in New Hyde Park?

Costs depend on room size, wall condition, and scope of work. Projects that include wallpaper removal or sheetrock installation will be quoted separately. Most standard painting rooms range from $800–$1,200. We provide free, transparent on-site estimates with no surprises.

Do you remove wallpaper in New Hyde Park?

Yes! Wallpaper removal is one of our specialties. Many New Hyde Park homes have wallpaper from previous decades. We use professional steamers to remove it completely, repair any wall damage, and prepare surfaces for a smooth paint finish.

Can you install sheetrock over old paneling?

Absolutely. We regularly install new sheetrock over dated wood paneling. This gives your walls a smooth, modern surface without the mess of full demolition. We handle the full process: installation, taping, mudding, sanding, priming, and painting.

Are you located near New Hyde Park?

Yes! Our headquarters is at 206 Mayfair Ave, West Hempstead, NY 11552 — just a short drive from New Hyde Park. We know the area well and have completed many projects in the village and surrounding neighborhoods.

How long does a wallpaper removal and painting project take?

A typical room with wallpaper removal takes 2–3 days including wall prep and painting. Larger projects with multiple rooms, sheetrock installation, or extensive prep may take 1–2 weeks. We'll provide a clear timeline during your free estimate.

What paint brands do you use?

We exclusively use Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams — the industry's best. We recommend low-VOC options that are safe for families and pets while delivering beautiful, long-lasting color.

Can you paint over wood paneling instead of removing it?

Yes, and the results are stunning. We caulk the seams, prep, prime with adhesion primer, and paint the paneling for a clean, modern finish. On a recent New Hyde Park project, we painted all the wall paneling in the family room and it came out beautiful — you'd never know it was paneling underneath.

Do you handle older homes with lead paint?

Yes, we're EPA Lead-Safe Certified. Many New Hyde Park homes built before 1978 may contain lead paint. We follow all safety protocols to protect your family during renovation and painting projects.
