Three Specialty Finishes, One Brooklyn Apartment

This Brooklyn homeowner came to us with a bold vision: every room needed its own personality. After a detailed color consultation, we mapped out a plan featuring three distinct specialty finishes — lime wash walls for the living areas, a warm golden drench color for the hallway, and deep plum accent walls for the home office.

The result is a one-of-a-kind apartment that feels both modern and timeless. Each transition between rooms creates a completely different atmosphere, tied together by consistent quality and clean craftsmanship throughout.

Preparation — The Foundation of Great Results

Specialty finishes demand flawless surfaces. Before any paint or plaster went on, our crew spent time thoroughly sanding every wall with our dustless vacuum system. This step is critical — especially for lime wash, where every imperfection shows through the translucent layers.

We covered all furniture with protective plastic sheeting, laid rosin paper on the floors, and masked off all trim and fixtures with blue painter's tape. This level of preparation is what separates a professional finish from a DIY job.

Our Crew at Work

Lime Wash — Handcrafted Texture with Natural Depth

Lime wash is one of the most sought-after specialty finishes. Unlike traditional paint, it's applied in thin, overlapping layers that create a cloud-like, organic texture. The color shifts subtly depending on the light and viewing angle, giving walls a living, breathing quality that flat paint simply cannot achieve.

For this Brooklyn apartment, we applied lime wash throughout the bedroom and living areas. The soft, neutral tone adds warmth and depth without overwhelming the space — perfect for a modern Brooklyn home.

Lime Wash Results

Drench Color — The Golden Hallway

Color drenching is one of the hottest interior design trends — a single color applied to every surface in a room, including walls, ceiling, doors, and trim. For this hallway, the homeowner chose a warm golden tone that envelops you as you walk through. The effect is striking: it transforms a simple corridor into an immersive experience.

Achieving a seamless drench color requires precision. Every edge where wall meets ceiling, every door frame, and every piece of trim must be painted in the same shade with consistent coverage. Our crew's experience with this technique ensured clean, professional results throughout.

Golden Drench Color Hallway

Deep Plum Office — Bold & Dramatic

The home office received the most dramatic treatment: a rich, deep plum shade applied across every surface, including the ceiling. This creates an intimate, focused atmosphere ideal for a workspace. The contrasting white trim on the doors provides a crisp frame that prevents the saturated color from feeling heavy.

Dark drench colors like this require extra coats and careful cutting in to achieve even coverage, especially where dark walls meet white trim. Our team delivered clean, sharp transitions throughout the room.
