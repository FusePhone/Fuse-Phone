Brooklyn Heights Painting FAQ

Do you have experience with landmarked Brooklyn Heights homes? +
Yes — Brooklyn Heights was New York City's first landmarked district. We regularly work on historic row houses and brownstones here. We understand plaster walls, original wood details, and the care these homes require. Our EPA Lead-Safe certification means we handle prewar surfaces properly.
How much does it cost to paint an apartment in Brooklyn Heights? +
Most rooms run $800–$1,200 depending on size and prep work needed. Every project is different — ceiling height, plaster condition, and scope all factor in. We provide a detailed written estimate broken down by room so you know exactly what you're paying for. No surprises, no hidden fees. All prices include premium Benjamin Moore paint, thorough prep, and our 2-year warranty.
Can you work with my co-op board's requirements? +
Absolutely. We provide Certificates of Insurance the same day, customized to your building. We coordinate elevator bookings, follow noise-hour restrictions, protect common areas, and clean up daily. Many Brooklyn Heights buildings have used us repeatedly because we know the process.
Is the paint safe for children? +
Yes. We use low-VOC and zero-VOC Benjamin Moore paints. Rooms are typically safe to sleep in the same night. For prewar homes with existing lead paint, we follow strict EPA containment procedures to prevent any dust exposure.
How long does a Brooklyn Heights brownstone floor take? +
A single parlor floor (living room, dining room, hallway) typically takes 3–5 days. A single room is 1–2 days. Full brownstone projects (all floors) can take 2–3 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline with your estimate.
Do you handle plaster repair before painting? +
Yes — it's one of our specialties. Brooklyn Heights homes often have plaster cracks, water damage spots, and areas needing skim coating. We repair everything before painting so you get a smooth, lasting finish. The repair cost is included in your estimate.
