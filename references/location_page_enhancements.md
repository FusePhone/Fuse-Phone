# Location Page Enhancement Spec

## Objective
Improve existing SEO page generation by enhancing sections with stronger SEO, better conversion, and more detailed content. DO NOT replace the current system. These are enhancements only.

## 1. Local Customer Review Section
Insert AFTER the Project Showcase section.

- 1 review per page
- Must mention the city naturally
- Must reference actual services (painting, drywall, cracks, etc.)
- Must sound like a real homeowner (not corporate)

Section Title: "What Homeowners in {City} Are Saying"

Example:
"We recently hired Gama Interior Painting for our home in {City}, and the experience was excellent from start to finish. The team handled everything professionally, from preparation to the final coat. We had several cracks in our walls and older surfaces that needed attention, and they repaired everything perfectly before painting. The final result looks amazing, and the entire process was clean and well organized. Highly recommend."
— Homeowner in {City}

Variation rules:
- Rotate phrasing across pages
- Keep tone natural and believable
- Do not reuse identical review text across cities

## 2. Upgraded Process Section
Replace the current simple 3-step process with a more detailed version.

Section Title: "Our Process"

1. **On-Site Estimate** — We visit your home, evaluate the space, and provide a clear, itemized estimate. In most cases, you will receive your estimate on the spot or within 24 hours.
2. **Preparation & Planning** — Our team protects all surfaces, prepares walls properly, repairs cracks and imperfections, and ensures everything is ready before applying paint.
3. **Professional Painting & Final Walkthrough** — We apply high-quality paint using updated techniques for a smooth, durable finish. Once completed, we walk through the project with you to ensure everything meets your expectations.

Always mention: itemized estimate, same day or 24h turnaround, surface preparation, clean work environment. Keep tone aligned with city personality (premium vs practical).

## 3. Expanded Services / Specialties Section
Section Title: "Our Painting & Surface Preparation Services"

Include:
- Interior Painting
- Cabinet Painting & Refinishing
- Drywall Repair
- Plaster Crack Repair & Surface Restoration
- Skim Coating (for smooth walls)
- Wallpaper Removal
- Trim, Doors, and Molding Painting

Supporting paragraph (vary per city):
"Many homes in {City} have older plaster walls, surface cracks, or previous finishes that require proper preparation before painting. Our team specializes in repairing cracks, smoothing surfaces, and restoring walls to a clean, modern finish before applying paint."

## 4. Local Keyword Integration
Naturally include variations of:
- painter {city}, interior painting {city}, painting company {city}, house painter {city}
- cabinet painting, drywall repair, plaster repair, skim coating

Spread across: hero, services, process, FAQ. Keep natural flow. No keyword stuffing.

## 5. Maintain Current System
Do NOT remove: SEO structure, scoring system, duplicate detection, variation engine, internal linking system, nearby areas logic, FAQ structure.

## 6. Duplication Control
- Review text varies across pages
- Services paragraph varies slightly
- Process wording adapts to tone_type
- If duplication threshold exceeded → rewrite affected sections

## 7. Output Quality
Each page must: feel more human and detailed, include real-world service explanations, clearly communicate process and value, include stronger trust signals (review + process clarity), maintain SEO integrity.
